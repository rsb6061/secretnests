# Reviews, Affiliates, and Disclosure Policy

Operational launch draft for counsel review.

SecretNests policy: never condition compensation, booking revenue share, visibility, or eligibility on positive or negative hotel sentiment; do not selectively solicit only expected-positive reviewers; do not edit reviews to change sentiment; treat negative and positive reviews under the same moderation rules; label demo content; disclose material connections including free/discounted stays, incentives, creator revenue share, hotel relationships, or other compensation.

The FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule took effect October 21, 2024. FTC guidance says incentives may be offered for reviews when they are not conditioned, expressly or implicitly, on a particular sentiment; material incentives/connections may still require clear disclosure under the FTC Act and Endorsement Guides.

Primary sources:
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/consumer-reviews-testimonials-rule-questions-answers
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/featuring-online-customer-reviews-guide-platforms
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/soliciting-paying-online-reviews-guide-marketers
- https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/advertising-marketing/endorsements-influencers-reviews

Suggested public disclosure: SecretNests may earn a commission when you book through certain links. Creators may receive a share of attributable booking revenue. Compensation is never tied to whether a hotel review is positive, negative, or favorable to a particular property.

For an incentivized or materially connected review, display a specific disclosure with that review/profile rather than relying only on a site-wide policy.
